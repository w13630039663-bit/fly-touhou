Methods & Causal Framework

  • Perception

    8-Channel Receptive Field

    LC4, LC11, LC9, and LPLC2 lobula projection neurons continuously extract looming threats and optical flow vectors.

  • Dynamics

    MaleCNS Reservoir

    80 neurons and 1,296 synapses solved via non-linear leaky tanh recurrent dynamics over 3 sub-steps per frame.

  • Actuation

    03 / Keyboard Rig

    16 descending neuron (DN) outputs decoded into real-time physical keypresses, manifested in the mechanical stepping rig.

  • Optimization

    CEM Neuroevolution

    Derivative-free cross-entropy evolution discovers robust zero-bias linear readout parameters without hand-tuned artificial fields.
